About / How to use

A search and browsing interface for OCR-transcribed Ottoman Turkish printed works. Page images are shown with their detected regions and lines; the transcriptions are searchable across one or all works.

How to use

  • Choose a work from the Works list in ⛃ Filters, or from the list on the left. Scroll the pages, or click a thumbnail.
  • Search any character, word or phrase in the search box. Options are under ⌕ Search ▾: regex for patterns (e.g. قر.ن), normalize to ignore ye/kef variants and ZWNJ, and the ⌨ keyboard.
  • Filters narrow the works in scope: works, regions, and metadata such as genre, author, publisher and year.
  • ❒ Browse regions (Filters, under Regions) lists every instance of the chosen region type, e.g. all footnotes or all images, across the works in scope. Step through them with ↑ / ↓.
  • ▦ Stats shows totals for the works in scope, and for a search: frequency over time and per work.
  • ⚙ Settings shows or hides regions and lines, and hides calibration charts (their page numbers stay as in the PDF).
  • Matched lines are highlighted on the page. Click a line to read its transcription. Drag the right edge of the left panel to resize it.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Enter / Shift+Enter in the search box: next / previous match. Esc: clear the search.
  • ↓ / ↑ or PageDown / PageUp: next / previous page (next / previous line when a line is selected).
  • → / ←: next / previous page.
  • + / −: zoom in / out. 0: fit width.
